About Haboye General Trading

A building materials supplier built on quality, reliability, and a genuine commitment to helping contractors and builders across Ethiopia succeed.

OUR STORY

Supplying What Ethiopia Builds With

Haboye General Trading PLC is a building materials supplier committed to raising the standard of construction supply in Ethiopia. We work with contractors, construction firms, and individual builders to make sure the essentials are available from a single, reliable source.

From steel and cement to finishing materials, every product we supply is chosen for quality before it reaches you. Our goal is simple: help you build with confidence.

Our Mission

To give every customer access to quality building materials, fair pricing, and dependable service.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted building materials suppliers in Ethiopia's construction industry.
